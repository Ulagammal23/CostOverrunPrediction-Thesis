**Overview**

The completed research plan represents the actual execution of the dissertation. The plan reflects the natural progression of tasks across the study, incorporating adjustments made during preprocessing, feature engineering, model development, interpretation, and dissertation writing. It is presented alongside the original planned timeline included in the research proposal, without altering the original document.

**Completed Research Plan**

The dissertation progressed through the following consolidated stages:

  - Selection of research topic and refinement of scope.
  - Extensive literature review to identify research gaps.
  - Preparation and submission of the research proposal.
  - Collection and integration of the BIM-AI dataset and FRED macroeconomic indicators.
  - Data preprocessing, transformation, and feature engineering.
  - Development and comparison of five machine learning models.
  - Hyperparameter tuning of all models using stratified cross-validation.
  - Validation-based selection of the best-performing model for both Model A and Model B.
  -  Final evaluation of selected models on the held-out test subset.
  - SHAP-based global and local interpretability analysis.
  - Chapter 4 implementation writing.
  - Chapter 5 results, analysis, and discussion.
  - Chapter 6 conclusions and future recommendations.
  - Iterative revisions based on supervisor feedback.
-  Final formatting, appendix preparation, and submission.

Adjustments were introduced during the implementation phase to strengthen methodological rigor. These included refinements to the model selection workflow, expansion of the SHAP interpretability section, integration of additional evaluation outputs, and structural updates to the dissertation chapters. These adjustments were consistent with the original research objectives.
